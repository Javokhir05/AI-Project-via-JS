Questions, answered

Is my chat history private?

In this version your account and history are stored in your own browser, so they stay on your device. A server-backed version would sync them across devices.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Every page adapts to small screens, and the history sidebar becomes a slide-in menu.

Can I send images?

Yes. Use the paperclip in the message box to attach an image and ask about it.

How do I delete my conversations?

Hover a chat in the sidebar and press the bin icon, or clear everything at once from the sidebar footer.
